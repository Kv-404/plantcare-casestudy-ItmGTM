# PlantCare

Home plant care for people who are at the office when the plants need water. ₹399 a month. Two visits. A photo the same day.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing to install. The date inside the site is Friday 2 October 2026. What you change stays in this browser.

The pages run in this order: Home, How it works, Societies, Pricing, Book, My plan. Care team is in the footer.

## Home

The offer is on the first screen. ₹399, two visits, and a sample note from a Thursday visit at Maple, flat A-1204. Under that, the two societies:

- Maple Residency, every Thursday. Next open day in the October list is Thu, 8 Oct.
- Cedar Heights, every Friday. Next open day is Fri, 9 Oct.
- Any other society joins a list. No visit, and no payment, until that building has its own weekday.

A visit is water, a light prune, a pest look, and a photo. Repotting, new plants, and sprays are not part of the price.

## How it works

You do not have to be home. The morning stops are flats a guard, a key, or a maid can open. A flat that opens only after 6 pm goes at the end of that building’s day.

If the door stays shut, that visit moves to the next Thursday or Friday for the same building. The other visit stays where it is.

Pause a month you are home. Those visits come off, and that month is not charged. Pay again and two new visits go on the next open days.

## Societies

Maple and Cedar are close enough that one day stays inside one building. A third society is not given a one-off visit, because the travel would cut how many flats get finished. The cap is 14 visits in a day, and that number holds only when every stop is in the same society.

## Pricing

₹399 for the month, two visits, about two weeks apart. Pause is free for that month. A building that is not on a route is not charged.

## Book

Pick the society, then the flat, the plant count, and how to get in.

- Maple books two Thursdays and takes ₹399.
- Cedar books two Fridays and takes ₹399.
- Another society, for example Lakeview Towers, saves the name only. The button says Join the list.

If that weekday already has 14 stops, the first visit moves to the next open week. The summary on the right shows the two dates before you submit. After booking, the site opens that flat under My plan.

## The case numbers

These are not on the public site.

Service cost is ₹170. Contribution is ₹399 − ₹170 = ₹229.

26 working days. (14 × 26) / 2 = 182 subscribers on one technician. 182 × ₹229 = ₹41,678.

A full-route salary is 182 × ₹170 = ₹30,940. Spread over a small list, that salary costs more than ₹399. The loss ends near 78 subscribers, because 30,940 / 399 is about 78.
