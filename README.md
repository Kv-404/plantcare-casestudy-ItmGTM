# PlantCare

The company site for the home plant-care subscription. ₹399 a month, two visits, for someone who is at the office when the plants need water. Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing to install.

The date inside the site is Friday 2 October 2026. What you change stays in this browser. The care team page can restore the original October list.

## What a resident sees

Home, How it works, Societies, and Pricing are the public pages. Book takes the ₹399.

- Maple Residency is visited on Thursday.
- Cedar Heights is visited on Friday.
- Any other society is saved on a list. No visit is placed, and no payment is taken.

A full day is 14 flats in one building. If that Thursday or Friday is already full, the first visit moves to the next open week.

## What the care team sees

The footer link Care team opens Ravi’s day book. One building, 14 stops. Open a stop, tick water, prune, pest look, and photo, write the note, and close it. That note shows on the member’s plan. If the door stays shut, write why and mark it. The stop moves to the next Thursday or Friday for that building.

Block the rest of this day fills the 14 so a new booking has to use the following week. Clear blocked slots removes those holds. Holds are not members.
